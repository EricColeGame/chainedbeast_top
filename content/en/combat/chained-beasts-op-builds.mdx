## What Makes a Build OP in Chained Beasts

"OP" in Chained Beasts rarely means a single broken combination. It means a build where every piece pushes in the same direction. Each beast carries a signature passive, and the skills you draft should feed that passive instead of fighting it.

Before you lock in an upgrade, run three quick checks:

- **Does it feed my beast's passive?** A special-attack bonus on Lion compounds; the same bonus on Armored Rhino mostly wastes a slot.
- **Does it create a window for my heavy option?** Stuns, trips, and blocks exist to set up a special attack, throw, or execution.
- **Does it still work if the shop rolls badly?** OP builds have a fallback plan, not just a dream loadout.

## The Best Chained Beasts OP Builds for Every Beast

The setups below focus on skill priority and in-fight decision-making. Player experience suggests that knowing *when* to use a skill matters more than squeezing in one extra damage node.

### Lion — Special Attack Burst

**Priority skills:** Lethal Targeting (critical damage), Soak Up the Roar (critical chance when the crowd is wild), Concussive Strength (longer stuns), plus any special attack damage upgrade.

**How to play:** Charge specials while enemies are stunned, tripped, or stuck in recovery frames, then clean up with melee. In co-op, ask a partner to set up the stun so you never charge into a live swing. Solo, learn the attack patterns first — Lion's payoff comes from patience, not aggression.

### Bear — Tank and Counterattack

**Priority skills:** Counter Spikes (damage on block), shield durability upgrades, and Hard Lessons for scaling damage as afflictions or skulls pile up.

**How to play:** Hold your ground, block the incoming string, and counter the moment the enemy overcommits. Bear's slow speed is the real weakness — chasing fast targets across the arena is how tank builds fall apart. In co-op, plant yourself in front of your damage dealers and let them work.

### Lioness — Chain Control

**Priority skills:** Sitting Ducks (bonus throw damage on tripped enemies), Grapple Master (pick up and throw stunned enemies), chain trip and squeeze upgrades, plus movement speed and stamina.

**How to play:** Use the longer chain to interrupt attacks and put enemies on the floor. Once something is down, either throw it into a hazard or step aside and let your team punish it. Lioness is the best "make your friends look good" beast in the game.

### Bone Collector Wolf — Dismemberment and Throws

**Priority skills:** Hard Lessons, Second Wind (offsets affliction drawbacks below 25% health), Grapple Master, and Bare Knuckle Specialist for an unarmed variation.

**How to play:** Harvest limbs constantly — the passive pays you twice, once in damage and once in cash. Throw severed parts at clustered enemies and grapple stunned targets into hazards. This is a high-risk build, so adjust your opening picks to whatever afflictions the run actually offers.

### Armored Rhino — Shield Tank

**Priority skills:** Counter Spikes, shield durability, flat damage reduction, and stamina upgrades so you can keep blocking.

**How to play:** Keep the shield up against anything scarier than you, wait out the full attack string, then counter or throw. Avoid burning stamina on panic dodges. For new players, this is the most forgiving way to learn enemy timing, and in co-op it lets Lion and Panther charge specials safely behind you.

### Agile Ibex — Dodge and Mobility

**Priority skills:** Stamina upgrades, critical-hit upgrades, and Sitting Ducks to convert trips into throws.

**How to play:** Never stand in front of an enemy longer than one exchange. Dodge through the swing, circle behind, and use chain trips to open throw windows. Always bank enough stamina to escape when three enemies close in at once — Ibex wins on timing, not toughness.

### Panther — Glass Cannon

**Priority skills:** Lethal Targeting, Untouchable (brief invulnerability after an execution), Second Wind, and Soak Up the Roar.

**How to play:** Trade nothing. Wait for the opening, dump specials into it, and execute weakened enemies to trigger Untouchable and reset the cycle. Panther pairs beautifully with a tank holding the frontline. Solo, invest early in a defensive option — the smaller health pool punishes one lazy dodge.

### Eagle — Economy Support

**Priority skills:** Loyalty (reduces friendly-fire damage), stamina upgrades, defensive skills, and crowd-favor skills that generate extra rewards.

**How to play:** Your job is to stay upright and keep the gold flowing. Play safe, maintain crowd favor with control tools, and funnel earnings into weapons, armor, and team upgrades. Eagle will not top the damage chart, but a well-funded team wins more runs.

The pattern is simple: solo builds buy insurance, co-op builds buy specialization. If you are queuing with strangers, lean toward self-sufficient chained beasts OP builds that do not depend on a teammate noticing your setup.

## FAQ

**What are the easiest chained beasts OP builds for beginners?**
Armored Rhino's shield tank and Bear's counterattack setup. Both forgive mistakes, teach you enemy timing, and stay useful even when the upgrade shop rolls badly.

**Can I force the same chained beasts OP builds every run?**
Not reliably. The arena hands out random upgrades, so treat every build as a priority list rather than a shopping list. Pick the closest available option and keep your three-part skeleton — damage, control, survival — intact.

**Which beast deals the most damage in Chained Beasts?**
Panther and Lion have the highest burst thanks to their special attack bonuses, while Bone Collector Wolf scales hardest in long fights once limb harvesting and Hard Lessons come online.

**Do these builds work in co-op?**
Yes, with one adjustment: shift a slot from survival into control or damage. A Lion with a reliable stun partner or a Panther behind an Armored Rhino will out-damage any solo version of the same setup.
