## Why the Chained Beasts System Requirements Matter More Than You'd Think

You've rounded up three friends, picked your gladiator, and you're ready to get chained together in the Colosseum — but first you want to know whether your rig can actually handle it. Chained Beasts system requirements are refreshingly modest on paper, yet a couple of lines in that spec sheet matter far more than the rest. Below we break down the Chained Beasts system requirements for Windows and macOS, translate each component into plain English, and explain the one accessory that decides whether your couch co-op night works at all.

The game launched on October 15, 2026, from developer and publisher Featherweight Games, and it's a 1–4 player co-op gladiator roguelike built around chaotic arena brawls. Because so much of the appeal is local and online multiplayer, "can my machine run it" is only half the question. The other half is "can my setup handle four people in one room."

## Chained Beasts System Requirements at a Glance

The [official Chained Beasts page on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3569420/Chained_Beasts/) lists two supported platforms — Windows and macOS — with a minimum spec for each. Notably, the "Recommended" section on the store page currently only restates the 64-bit processor and operating system requirement, so the minimum tier is effectively the only hard baseline you have to work from.

| Component | Windows minimum | macOS minimum |
|---|---|---|
| OS | Windows 10 (64-bit) | macOS 11 |
| Processor | Intel Core i5-5257U or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 (or equivalent) | Apple M1 or Intel Core i7-8850H |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM | 8 GB RAM |
| Graphics | GeForce GTX 1050 or AMD RX 460 (or equivalent) | Apple M1 or Radeon Pro 560X |
| Architecture | 64-bit processor and OS required | 64-bit processor and OS required |
| Additional notes | At least 1 gamepad required for same-screen co-op | At least 1 gamepad required for same-screen co-op |

Two things jump out. First, 8 GB of RAM on both platforms is a low bar by modern standards — most gaming laptops and desktops from the last several years clear it without thinking. Second, the controller note isn't a suggestion, it's a requirement for split-screen play. More on that below.

## Breaking Down Each Spec in Plain English

Spec sheets are written for hardware reviewers, not players. Here's what each line actually means for someone who just wants to throw an urn at a gladiator.

| Component | Listed requirement | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Processor | Intel Core i5-5257U / Ryzen 3 1200 | A modest dual-core-era Intel laptop chip or an entry-level AMD desktop chip. Almost any gaming CPU from the past five years clears this comfortably. |
| Graphics | GTX 1050 / RX 460 | Entry-level discrete GPUs from several generations back. Integrated graphics are the real question mark here. |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM | Fine for the game alone; if you're streaming or running a browser with 40 tabs, you'll want 16 GB. |
| OS | Windows 10 / macOS 11 | Both are older baselines, so most players are already covered. |
| 64-bit | Required | Standard on effectively every modern system. Non-issue. |

The takeaway: this is a top-down, stylized arena brawler, not a photorealistic open world. The graphics target is deliberately forgiving, which is good news for laptop owners and anyone playing on older hardware.

### What About Integrated Graphics?

The store page lists discrete GPUs as the minimum, so integrated graphics are technically outside the official support window. That said, community reports from players with modern integrated chips suggest the game is playable at lower settings, though results vary wildly by chip generation. If you're on integrated graphics, use the free demo before buying — the store page confirms a demo is available.

## macOS Support and Apple Silicon

Mac players got a genuinely modern baseline here. The macOS minimum lists the Apple M1 chip directly, which means native Apple Silicon support rather than a Rosetta-only experience. If you're on an M-series MacBook Air or Mac mini, you're at or above the minimum tier out of the box.

| Mac type | Chip listed | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) | Apple M1 | Meets or exceeds minimum |
| Intel Mac with discrete graphics | Core i7-8850H + Radeon Pro 560X | Meets minimum if you match both parts |
| Older Intel Mac with integrated graphics | Not listed | Likely below minimum — test the demo first |

The Intel Mac path is the tricky one. You need to match *both* the listed CPU and GPU, and a lot of older Intel MacBooks have integrated graphics that fall short of the Radeon Pro 560X reference. Check your exact model's specs before you commit.

## The Controller Requirement Nobody Reads Carefully

This is the line that quietly ruins co-op nights: **at least one gamepad is required for same-screen co-op.** The developers also state that they recommend playing with a controller generally.

| Play mode | Players | Controller needed? |
|---|---|---|
| Single-player | 1 | Recommended, not strictly required |
| Local same-screen co-op | 2–4 | Yes — at least one gamepad, and realistically one per player |
| Online co-op | 2–4 | Recommended |
| Remote Play Together | 2–4 | Recommended; host needs a stable connection |

If you're planning a four-player couch session, assume you need four controllers. Sharing a single pad across four chained gladiators isn't going to work, and mixing keyboard and gamepad across a split-screen setup is where a lot of player experience reports mention friction. Both Xbox and PlayStation controllers are listed as supported.

## How to Check Your Rig Before You Buy

You don't need a benchmarking suite. Five minutes in Windows or macOS settings will tell you everything.

| Step | Where to look | What you're checking |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Confirm 64-bit | Settings → System → About | System type says "64-bit operating system" |
| 2. Check your CPU | Task Manager → Performance → CPU | Model name matches or beats the listed chip |
| 3. Check your GPU | Task Manager → Performance → GPU | Dedicated GPU listed, not just integrated |
| 4. Check RAM | Task Manager → Performance → Memory | 8 GB or more installed |
| 5. Confirm OS version | Settings → System → About | Windows 10 or newer; macOS 11 or newer on Mac |

On macOS, hold Option and click the Apple menu → System Information to see your chip, memory, and graphics in one place. If everything checks out, the next step is the demo — it's the cheapest possible compatibility test and it costs nothing but download time.

## Performance Tips and What the Community Is Saying

Because the game is a roguelike with permadeath elements, a mid-run crash stings more than usual. A few practical habits help:

- **Close background apps before a long run.** With only 8 GB as the baseline, browser tabs and overlays eat into headroom that the arena wants.
- **Cap your frame rate.** A stable, slightly lower frame rate beats a fluctuating high one in a game where timing your chain pulls matters.
- **Use a wired connection for online co-op.** Four players chained together means constant position syncing; Wi-Fi jitter shows up as rubber-banding.
- **Verify your install after patches.** The developers posted shortly after launch that they were already working on fixes, balance changes, and progression improvements, so updates are arriving regularly.

On Steam Deck compatibility, the store page currently lists the status as **Unknown**, so there's no official verification verdict yet. Community reports are mixed and anecdotal — treat handheld play as an experiment rather than a guarantee until Valve or the developers publish a rating.

Worth noting for context: the game sits at a "Very Positive" overall rating across roughly 655 user reviews, with about 83% positive. That's a healthy signal that performance isn't a widespread complaint, though individual reports always vary by hardware.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are the Chained Beasts system requirements hard to meet?**
No — they're on the modest side. An entry-level discrete GPU, 8 GB of RAM, and a mid-range CPU from the last several years will clear the Windows minimum. The bigger practical hurdle is having enough controllers for local co-op.

**Can I run Chained Beasts on a laptop?**
Probably, if your laptop has a dedicated GPU like a GTX 1050 or better and at least 8 GB of RAM. Laptops with only integrated graphics sit outside the official spec, so test the free demo before purchasing.

**Do I need a controller to play?**
For same-screen co-op, yes — the store page explicitly requires at least one gamepad. The developers also recommend a controller for the game generally, and Xbox and PlayStation pads are both supported.

**Is there a Mac version, and does it support Apple Silicon?**
Yes. The macOS minimum lists macOS 11 with an Apple M1 or Intel i7-8850H, and the Apple M1 appears again as the graphics baseline — which points to native Apple Silicon support rather than emulation.

## Final Check Before You Enter the Arena

The Chained Beasts system requirements aren't going to be the thing that stops most people from playing. A GTX 1050-class GPU, 8 GB of RAM, and a 64-bit OS cover the vast majority of gaming PCs built in the last several years, and Apple Silicon Macs are supported natively out of the box. What will stop you is showing up to a four-player couch session with one controller and a dream.

So do the boring part first: check your GPU and RAM, confirm your OS version, count your gamepads, and download the demo. Then chain up, coordinate your pulls, and enjoy the chaos — the arena is a lot more fun when the only thing breaking is your gladiator's leg, not your frame rate.
