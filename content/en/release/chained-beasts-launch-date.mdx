## What Shipped on Day One When the Chained Beasts Launch Date Arrived

Launch-day content is often thinner than trailers suggest, so it is worth being specific about what actually landed on October 15. Chained Beasts shipped as a complete roguelite loop: run-based arena combat, escalating bosses, persistent injuries that carry between fights, and a crowd-favor economy that rewards showboating as much as efficiency.

The injury system is the standout hook. Instead of simply losing health, your gladiator accumulates lasting wounds — a limp that slows your movement, a missing eye that narrows your vision, or hallucinations only your character can see. Potions exist, but side effects can stack new afflictions on top of the old ones, which makes every healing decision a gamble rather than a reset button.

Progression is handled through a mix of in-run purchases and longer-term unlocks. You earn crowd favor and coin, spend it on weapons and equipment between bouts, and gradually unlock new characters with distinct abilities. According to player experience shared in Steam reviews, no two runs follow the same path, which is exactly what the roguelite structure is supposed to deliver.

## Is It Worth Playing After the Chained Beasts Launch Date?

Short answer: yes, if you have a regular group. Chained Beasts is fundamentally a social game. The chain mechanic forces proximity, which means every fight becomes a negotiation about positioning, and every mistimed dash yanks a teammate into a spike wall. Playing solo works, but you lose the part that makes the game memorable.

### Practical Tips for Your First Run

- **Assign a chain leader.** One player calls the movement direction; everyone else follows. It sounds tedious and it saves runs.
- **Use the environment before your weapons.** Urns, spike walls, and snake pits do more damage than your starting sword.
- **Do not hoard potions.** Side effects stack, but so do untreated wounds. Drink early and manage the consequences.
- **Throw weapons deliberately.** Ranged throws are strong, but you lose the weapon — keep a backup or plan to fight bare-handed.
- **Bank crowd favor.** Winning over the audience pays out between bouts. Showboat when it is safe, not when a boss is winding up.
- **Play the demo with your actual group.** Chain coordination depends on how your specific friends behave under pressure.
