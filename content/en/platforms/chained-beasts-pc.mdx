### What the Specs Mean in Practice

- **GTX 1050 / RX 460 class graphics** are entry-level by modern standards, which fits a top-down, stylized arena game. Don't count on headroom for high-resolution play on a large 4K display.
- **8 GB of RAM is the listed minimum**, so close browsers, capture software, and launchers before a four-player session.
- **A 64-bit OS is mandatory** on both platforms — Windows 10 or macOS 11 at the very least.
- **At least one gamepad is required for same-screen co-op**, per the store listing. Plan your controller count before you plan the pizza.
- **Steam Deck compatibility was listed as "Unknown"** when this was written, so verify before committing to handheld play.

## Controller Setup and Performance Tips for Chained Beasts PC

The developers recommend a controller, and for local play it edges toward mandatory. Because the store page explicitly notes that at least one gamepad is needed for same-screen co-op, a couch session without pads is a non-starter. Sort out your input plan before the group arrives.

| Play scenario | Input setup | What to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo run | Gamepad or keyboard and mouse | Controller is the developer-recommended option |
| Local split-screen | At least one gamepad required | Match pad count to player count for the smoothest setup |
| Online co-op | Each player uses their own device | Cross-play is supported across platforms |
| Remote Play Together | Host's copy is shared | Guests can join without purchasing the game |

A few practical tips for a smoother **Chained Beasts PC** session:

- **Benchmark with the free demo first.** It's the cheapest way to confirm your machine handles four-player chaos before you spend anything.
- **Use a wired connection for online co-op.** Tether physics already cause plenty of accidental collisions without latency adding to the confusion.
- **Test split-screen with two players before inviting four.** It's far easier to diagnose a controller mapping problem with one friend than with three.
- **Keep Steam Cloud enabled.** Roguelite progression is slow-burn; losing unlocks to a reinstall stings.
- **Trim background apps.** With an 8 GB RAM floor, browser tabs and capture tools compete directly with the game.

## FAQ

**Is Chained Beasts PC playable solo?**
Yes. Single-player is a supported mode, so you can learn the arena and the chain physics before bringing friends along. That said, the game is designed around co-op chaos, and most of the appeal comes from shared disasters.

**Does Chained Beasts PC support cross-platform multiplayer?**
Yes. The store listing confirms cross-platform multiplayer alongside online co-op for up to four players, so mixed-platform groups can play together. Remote Play Together also lets a friend join your session without owning a copy.

**Will my computer run Chained Beasts PC?**
Check the minimums: Windows 10 or macOS 11, 8 GB of RAM, and a GTX 1050 / RX 460-class GPU or Apple M1. No recommended spec is published, so the minimums are your realistic baseline. The free demo is the most reliable test.

Yes — a free demo is available on Steam. It's a sensible way to verify performance and confirm that your group finds the chain mechanic fun rather than infuriating.
