## Practical Tips for Chained Beasts Xbox Controller Players

Getting the most out of the game with a pad comes down to a handful of habits. These are the ones that make the biggest difference in the first few hours.

- **Tighten your stick dead zones.** Chain physics reward small, deliberate inputs. A wide dead zone makes tugging feel mushy, so lower it in Steam Input.
- **Use wired controllers for local co-op.** Two or more wireless pads in one room invite interference and dropped inputs at the worst possible moment.
- **Assign controller order before launching.** Steam's controller settings let you lock player slots, which prevents the classic split-screen mix-up where player two inherits player one's pad.
- **Rebind grab and throw to comfortable buttons.** Throwing weapons and hurling enemies are core mechanics; put them where your thumb naturally rests.
- **Talk through chain tension.** The single biggest cause of tangled failure is two players pulling in opposite directions. Call your movement before you commit.
- **Spend crowd favor early.** Winning over the audience pays out consistently, and the earlier you lean into it, the smoother the boss fights feel.

| Common issue | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controller not detected | Bluetooth pairing or Steam Input disabled | Re-pair the pad, enable Steam Input |
| Second pad ignored in split-screen | Controller order not assigned | Set player slots in Steam controller settings |
| Noticeable input lag | Wireless interference | Switch to USB-C or a 2.4 GHz dongle |
| Chains feel unresponsive | Dead zone too wide | Reduce dead zone per stick |
