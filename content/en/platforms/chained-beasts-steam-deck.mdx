## Controls: A Gamepad-First Game in Your Hands

Featherweight Games explicitly recommends playing with a controller, and the store page notes that at least one gamepad is required for same-screen co-op. That is good news for the Steam Deck, which has built-in sticks, triggers, and trackpads — but there are still a few things to set up.

- **Use the official layout first.** Launch the game, open the Steam overlay, and check whether a developer-made controller layout appears before building your own.
- **Assign the trackpad as a mouse.** Menus and inventory screens in top-down games often behave better with pointer input.
- **Keep the back buttons free.** Binding them to dodge or throw is far more comfortable than stretching for face buttons mid-fight.
- **Set controller order deliberately.** If you dock the Deck and add external pads for split-screen, reorder devices in the Quick Access menu so player one stays on the handheld.

1. **Check the store page status.** Confirm whether the compatibility badge has changed since launch.
