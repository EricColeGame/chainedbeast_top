Few beasts in the arena hit as hard — or fold as fast — as the Panther. One clean special attack can end a fight, but one mistimed dodge can end your run, which is exactly why the **chained beasts panther build** is the difference between a highlight reel and a quick defeat. This guide breaks down the skills, upgrade order, and combat loop that make the **chained beasts panther build** work in both solo and co-op play, without pretending the Panther is something it isn't.

## Why the Panther Is Built to Hit First and Hit Hard

The Panther's identity is pure offense. It starts with higher base damage than most of the roster, and its passive adds a 50% bonus to special attack damage — a huge multiplier that is paid for with a noticeably smaller health pool. The result is a beast that wins exchanges in seconds or loses them just as quickly.

| Trait | Panther | What It Means in Practice |
|---|---|---|
| Base damage | High | Normal attacks already trade well against most enemies |
| Special attack bonus | +50% | Charged specials are your win condition, not a bonus |
| Health pool | Low | Two or three sloppy exchanges can end the run |
| Ideal engagement | Short and brutal | Get in, unload, reposition before the crowd closes in |

That profile shapes every decision below. A tank build that slowly grinds enemies down fights against the Panther's strengths; a burst build that ends fights before the enemy swings leans into them. If you have played defensive beasts like the Armored Rhino or the Bear first, expect an adjustment period — the Panther punishes patience almost as much as it punishes greed.

## Best Skills for the Chained Beasts Panther Build

Skills in this game are not guaranteed every run, so treat the list below as a priority order rather than a shopping list. When your ideal pick doesn't appear, take the next item that supports burst damage or keeps you alive long enough to land it.

| Skill | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Lethal Targeting** | Increases critical damage | Must-pick |
| **Untouchable** | Grants brief invulnerability after an execution | Must-pick |
| **Soak Up the Roar** | Improves critical chance when the crowd is wild | High |
| **Second Wind** | Softens affliction drawbacks once health drops below 25% | Situational |
| **Turn the Tides** | Blocked projectiles land at your feet and your next hit crits | Alternative |
| **Drive It Home** | Extra melee damage when you punch an embedded weapon free | Alternative |

The logic behind this order is simple. **Lethal Targeting** multiplies the damage of the hits you already want to land, and **Untouchable** converts a successful execution into a free escape window — the two skills cover both halves of the glass-cannon equation. **Soak Up the Roar** is the crowd-favor payoff that keeps crit chance high during chaotic multi-enemy fights, which is exactly when the Panther is most likely to die.

**Second Wind** deserves a note of caution. It is a safety net for affliction-heavy runs, not a license to fight at low health. Take it when your run is already stacking risky afflictions; skip it when you are playing clean and would rather have another damage multiplier.

## Upgrade Priorities: Early, Mid, and Late Run

Knowing which skills to grab is only half of a **chained beasts panther build**. The other half is knowing when to spend your upgrades on damage and when to buy insurance. The table below reflects a practical progression most players can follow without needing a perfect roll.

| Run Phase | Priority | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Early (first few fights) | Critical chance, then critical damage | Crit stats scale both normal and special attacks |
| Mid (arena momentum building) | Special attack damage, stamina | Bigger burst windows and enough gas to reposition |
| Late (heavy enemies, crowded arenas) | Execution tools, health, Second Wind | Survive the one hit you cannot dodge |
| Any phase | Movement and dodge support | Staying mobile is the Panther's real armor |

A common trap is over-investing in raw special attack damage while ignoring stamina. The Panther's damage ceiling means nothing if you cannot disengage after a combo. Treat stamina as a damage stat: every dodge you can afford is a combo you get to start.

## The Panther Combat Loop: Burst, Execute, Reset

The Panther does not win long exchanges. It wins short ones, repeated. Community reports and player experience consistently describe the same rhythm: bait an attack, punish the recovery, execute the weakened target, then move before the rest of the pack arrives.

| Situation | Best Response |
|---|---|
| Enemy is mid-attack | Dodge through or around the swing, then counter |
| Enemy is staggered or recovering | Land your charged special immediately |
| Enemy is low health and stunned | Execute for the kill plus the Untouchable window |
| Multiple enemies closing in | Disengage, reset spacing, look for an isolated target |
| Crowd favor is peaking | Push for crit-heavy specials while Soak Up the Roar is active |

Two habits separate clean Panther runs from messy ones. First, never open a fight with your special — open with a dodge or a light hit to confirm the enemy's recovery timing, then commit. Second, always know your exit. If you cannot name the direction you will dodge after the combo lands, you are not ready to start it.

## Solo vs. Co-op: Adjusting the Build

The same **chained beasts panther build** plays differently depending on who is standing next to you. Solo, you are the only one who can absorb a mistake, so defensive picks earn their slot. In co-op, you can hand off that job entirely.

| Setting | What Changes | Recommended Adjustments |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | No one to peel enemies off you | Take Second Wind or health upgrades earlier; play more conservatively |
| Co-op with a tank | Someone else holds the frontline | Skip defensive picks, stack crit and special damage |
| Co-op with a controller | Lioness or Wolf sets up trips and stuns | Add throw-damage and grapple skills to punish held enemies |
| Co-op with another Panther | Damage is abundant, survivability is not | One Panther should lean defensive or split targets deliberately |

The strongest pairing is a Panther behind an Armored Rhino or Bear. The tank draws the swing, the Panther punishes the recovery, and the fight ends before the enemy's second attack. A Lioness partner works almost as well, because a tripped enemy is a free special attack.

## Common Mistakes That Sink Panther Runs

Most failed Panther runs share the same handful of causes. Fixing them is usually faster than hunting for a better skill roll.

- **Trading hits on purpose.** The Panther loses almost every even trade. If you are standing still and swinging, you are already losing.
- **Hoarding special attacks.** A charged special that never lands deals zero damage. Use it on a confirmed opening, not a perfect one.
- **Skipping executions.** Executions remove a threat and, with Untouchable, buy you a free escape. They are not just a finisher.
- **Ignoring the crowd.** Crowd favor feeds crit chance through skills like Soak Up the Roar. Safe, flashy play keeps the multiplier alive.
- **Building like a tank.** Health and shields are insurance, not an identity. If your **chained beasts panther build** is out-surviving its damage output, you have drifted too far from the plan.
- **Fighting in open space.** Corners and arena edges kill Panthers. Keep a lane open behind you at all times.

## FAQ

**Is the chained beasts panther build good for beginners?**
It is playable but unforgiving. The Panther's low health pool means early mistakes cost entire runs, so many players start with the Lion or Armored Rhino to learn enemy timing, then switch to the Panther once dodging feels automatic.

**What are the two must-have skills for the Panther?**
Lethal Targeting and Untouchable. Lethal Targeting raises critical damage, and Untouchable grants brief invulnerability after an execution, which turns a kill into a safe exit. Everything else is flexible depending on what the run offers.

**Can the Panther work in solo play?**
Yes, with adjustments. Solo Panthers should pick up survivability earlier than co-op Panthers, avoid fighting multiple enemies at once, and treat stamina upgrades as a damage investment rather than a luxury.

**Which beast pairs best with the Panther in co-op?**
A frontline tank such as the Armored Rhino or Bear. They absorb the attacks the Panther cannot, creating the recovery windows a burst build needs to land its specials safely.
