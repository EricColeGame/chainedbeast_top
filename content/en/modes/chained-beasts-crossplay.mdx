## What Chained Beasts Crossplay Actually Covers

Chained Beasts is a 1–4 player co-op gladiator roguelike from Featherweight Games, released on October 15, 2026. You and up to three allies are chained together inside a Roman arena, and those chains are not cosmetic. They drag, they snag on pillars, and they can be used to trip enemies or swing a partner into a wall of spikes.

The [official Chained Beasts page on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3569420/Chained_Beasts/) lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer as a confirmed feature, sitting alongside Online Co-Op, Shared/Split Screen Co-op, and Remote Play Together. In plain terms, Chained Beasts crossplay support means your group isn't locked to one device family — a PC player and a friend on a different platform can share the same lobby.

| Feature | What It Does | Who It's For |

| Cross-Platform Multiplayer | Lets players on different platforms join the same session | Groups split across devices |

One caveat worth repeating: platform lists change with patches and storefront rollouts. Before you plan a crossplay night, check the store page's feature list and the developer's announcements rather than trusting a months-old forum post.

## Every Chained Beasts Multiplayer Mode Compared

Not all Chained Beasts crossplay sessions feel the same, because the game supports several connection types. Community reports consistently say online lobbies are the most flexible, while split-screen is the most chaotic — mostly because four people shouting chain instructions in one room is a different experience from four people on headsets.

| Mode | Players | Connection Type | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Online Co-Op | 1–4 | Internet lobby | Long progression runs with friends |
| Cross-Platform Multiplayer | 1–4 | Mixed platforms | Groups with different hardware |
| Shared/Split Screen | 2–4 | Local, one machine | Couch co-op nights |
| Remote Play Together | 2–4 | Streamed local session | Inviting a friend who hasn't bought it |

Two practical notes from the system requirements: same-screen co-op needs at least one gamepad connected, and the developers recommend playing with a controller overall. If you're planning a mixed Chained Beasts crossplay group, ask everyone to have a pad on hand before the session starts.

- **Online co-op** rewards patience — one bad connection can yank the whole chain sideways.
- **Split-screen** is the fastest way to learn chain physics, because you can see your partner's screen.
- **Remote Play Together** is excellent for demo nights, but streaming latency makes precise chain swings harder.
- **Crossplay lobbies** work best when everyone has a stable wired or strong Wi-Fi connection.

## How to Set Up a Chained Beasts Crossplay Session

Setup is straightforward, but small oversights cause most failed lobbies. Work through these steps in order and you'll skip the twenty-minute troubleshooting phase that eats into arena time.

| Step | What to Do | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Update the game on every device | Crossplay often requires matching builds |
| 2 | Confirm each player's platform supports crossplay | Older builds or regions may differ |
| 3 | Connect a controller where local play is involved | Same-screen co-op requires at least one gamepad |
| 4 | Pick one host with the strongest connection | Host stability affects chain physics for everyone |
| 5 | Invite through the in-game lobby, not platform invites alone | Cross-platform friends may not appear in native lists |
| 6 | Run a short practice arena before a real run | Confirms everyone can see and drag the chain |

A tip from player experience: do a two-minute chain test in the first arena. Have each player walk in a different direction, then try a synchronized drag on a training dummy. If the chain rubber-bands, fix the connection before you commit to a run — dying because a teammate teleported mid-swing feels worse than any boss.

## Chained Beasts Crossplay vs. Local Co-op: Which Should You Pick?

Both options are legitimate, and the right choice depends on how serious your group is about progression. Crossplay opens the roster to friends anywhere; local play removes latency entirely but limits you to one room.

| Criteria | Crossplay Online | Local Split-Screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Latency | Depends on host and connections | None |
| Group size flexibility | High — friends anywhere | Limited to one household |
| Chain coordination | Voice chat required | Shouting across a couch works |
| Progression sharing | Each player keeps their own unlocks | Shared screen, shared chaos |
| Best for | Long-term roguelite grinding | Party nights and new players |

If your group is chasing deeper runs, Chained Beasts crossplay is the better long-term setup. Injuries, potions, and permanent unlocks reward consistent teams who learn each other's habits, and that's much easier when you can play with the same four people regardless of what they own.

## Tips for Keeping a Crossplay Team Alive

The chain is a shared resource, and most wipes come from one player moving when the group isn't ready. Treat movement like a turn-based decision: call the direction, count it, then commit.

- **Assign a chain caller.** One player announces drags, swings, and retreats so nobody guesses.
- **Use the environment, not just weapons.** Urns, spike walls, and snake pits kill faster than a sword swing — and the crowd loves it.
- **Bank crowd favor early.** Winning over the audience pays out over time, and happy crowds are more forgiving of sloppy runs.
- **Manage wounds together.** Limps, lost eyes, and hallucinations stack up daily; potions help but can add worse afflictions, so discuss surgery as a team.
- **Rotate who leads the chain.** A fixed lead gets predictable, and bosses punish predictability.

Community reports also suggest that crossplay groups should agree on a session length before starting. Roguelike runs sprawl, and a teammate dropping mid-boss because of a schedule conflict is the most avoidable death in the game.

## FAQ

**Does Chained Beasts crossplay work between PC and consoles?**
The Steam store page lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer as an official feature, which confirms crossplay exists in some form. The exact platform matrix can change with patches, so verify the current list on the store page before planning a session with friends on other hardware.

**How many players can join a Chained Beasts crossplay lobby?**
Up to four. Chained Beasts is built as a 1–4 player co-op gladiator roguelike, and the chained movement system is designed around a full party of four ferocious friends.

**Do I need a controller for Chained Beasts crossplay?**
Not for online play, but the developers recommend a controller, and same-screen co-op requires at least one gamepad. If your group mixes local and online players, have pads ready.

**Is Chained Beasts crossplay the same as Remote Play Together?**
No. Remote Play Together streams a local session to a friend who doesn't own the game, while Chained Beasts crossplay connects players who each own a copy on their own platform. Remote Play is a demo tool; crossplay is the real progression setup.
