## What Makes Chained Beasts Multiplayer Different

Chained Beasts multiplayer turns a brawler into a coordination test: up to four gladiators, one shared chain, and an arena that punishes anyone who sprints in the wrong direction. Featherweight Games released the co-op gladiator roguelike on October 15, 2026, and the feature players keep talking about is not the blood or the bosses — it is how the chain rewires teamwork. If you are planning a Chained Beasts multiplayer session with friends, this guide covers every supported mode, how each squad size feels, and the habits that keep a group alive past the first real boss.

The core pitch is simple: you are tethered to your teammates, and the tether is a weapon as much as a handicap. That means Chained Beasts multiplayer is less about individual skill expression and more about synchronized chaos.

### Online Co-Op

Online co-op is the default way most groups will play. You host a lobby, invite friends through Steam, and the run begins. Because Chained Beasts is a roguelite, progress is run-based: you learn the arena layout, bank some permanent unlocks, and push further next time. Player experience suggests voice chat is close to mandatory — the chain punishes silent teammates far more than it punishes weak ones.

### Local and Split-Screen Co-Op

Shared/split-screen support makes this one of the better couch co-op picks of the year. Note the system requirement: at least one gamepad is needed for same-screen play, and the developers recommend controllers generally. If you are planning a four-player living-room night, confirm you have enough pads before everyone arrives.

### Remote Play Together and Cross-Platform

Remote Play Together lets a host stream the game to friends who do not own it, which is ideal for demoing the chaos. Cross-platform multiplayer is listed as a supported feature, though specifics can vary — test a lobby with your group before committing to a long session.

## How Squad Size Changes a Run

Squad size is not just a difficulty slider. Each extra gladiator adds damage output and adds tether complexity in roughly equal measure. Community reports consistently describe two-player runs as the cleanest way to learn the chain and four-player runs as the funniest way to lose one.

| Squad size | Chain pressure | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 player | None | Pure roguelike fundamentals: dodge, throw, manage wounds alone |
| 2 players | Low | Easiest to synchronize; ideal for learning boss timings together |
| 3 players | Medium | Positioning starts to matter; one player often becomes the anchor |
| 4 players | High | Maximum damage and maximum tangles; rewards a caller and clear roles |

A practical takeaway: run your first two or three attempts at two players. Once both of you can clear the early arena without snagging on pillars, invite the rest of the group. You will spend far less time untangling and more time actually fighting.

## Setting Up a Session: A Step-by-Step Checklist

Multiplayer problems in Chained Beasts are usually setup problems, not game problems. Work through this before your group logs on.

| Step | Action | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Download the free demo and run one solo match | Confirms performance before you commit four players |
| 2 | Check controllers and pairing | Same-screen co-op needs at least one gamepad |
| 3 | Agree on a voice channel | The chain demands live callouts |
| 4 | Pick a host with the best connection | Reduces desync complaints during boss fights |
| 5 | Set a session goal | "Reach the second boss" beats "play until we quit" |
| 6 | Assign a caller | One voice for direction prevents four people pulling four ways |
| 7 | Review the wound system together | New players panic at injuries that are actually survivable |

**System requirements to keep in mind (Windows minimum):** Windows 10, 8 GB RAM, and a GPU in the GeForce GTX 1050 / AMD RX 460 class. macOS players need macOS 11 with an Apple M1 or Intel i7-8850H class chip. The bar is modest, which is one reason the game runs well in shared-screen setups.

## Chain Tactics That Win Arenas

Chained Beasts multiplayer rewards a small number of habits repeated consistently. The developers built environmental kills into the design — urns, spike walls, snake pits — and those tools are far easier to use when the squad moves as one unit.

| Role | Job | Chain responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caller | Sets direction, calls targets | Keeps the group moving one way |
| Anchor | Holds position near hazards | Gives the chain a stable pivot point |
| Thrower | Uses urns, weapons, and enemies as projectiles | Ranged pressure without breaking formation |
| Finisher | Commits to downed and staggered foes | Cleans up while others reposition |

**Habits that separate good squads from tangled ones:**

- **Move in arcs, not lines.** Straight-line sprints drag teammates into pillars and spike walls.
- **Announce before you throw.** A hurled urn that misses is a wasted turn; one that hits a friend is a disaster.
- **Use the chain offensively.** A coordinated pull can trip enemies, not just teammates.
- **Treat wounds as build choices.** A limp or a missing eye changes how you fight — lean into it instead of fighting it. Potions help, but side effects can stack, so read before you drink.
- **Bank money for gear.** Weapons and equipment bought between fights swing more runs than raw aggression does.
- **Rotate unlockable characters.** Each new gladiator brings abilities that change what your squad can attempt.

The launch update from the developer noted that tens of thousands of gladiators entered the arena in the first week and that fixes, balance changes, and progression improvements are in the pipeline. Translation for your group: patch notes are worth a quick read before a long session, because balance shifts can change which tactics still work.

## FAQ

**Can you play Chained Beasts multiplayer alone?**
Yes. The game supports 1–4 players, so a solo run is a legitimate way to learn boss patterns, test weapons, and unlock characters before your friends join. Many players treat solo attempts as practice for group nights.

**How many players can join a Chained Beasts multiplayer session?**
Up to four. Squad size is chosen per run, and the experience changes noticeably between two, three, and four players — mostly in how much coordination the chain demands.

**Does Chained Beasts multiplayer support cross-platform play?**
The store page lists cross-platform multiplayer as a feature, along with online co-op, shared/split-screen, and Remote Play Together. Because platform support details can shift with patches, run a short test lobby with your group before scheduling a long session.

**Do you need a controller for local co-op?**
The system requirements state that at least one gamepad is required for same-screen co-op, and the developers recommend playing with a controller overall. If you are setting up a four-player couch session, gather enough pads in advance.

**Is there a demo?**
Yes — a free demo is available, which is the cheapest way to confirm the chain mechanic clicks with your group before everyone buys in. The full game is listed at $19.99, with launch discounts appearing periodically.

**Is the game gory?**
The developers describe visible blood sprays that remain in the environment. It is stylized rather than realistic, but worth knowing if you are planning a session with younger players.
